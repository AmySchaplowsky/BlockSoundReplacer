Block Sound Replacer (Valheim, BepInEx)

Replaces the Staff of Protection barrier hit sound and the parry (perfect block) sound with hitsound.wav.
Config: BepInEx\config\local.blocksoundreplacer.cfg  (delete it after updating from an older version)
  ReplaceBarrierHit (default on), ReplaceParry (default on), ReplaceBlock (default off), DirectPlayback
Build + install: double-click build.bat (needs .NET SDK and BepInEx installed).
Parry debugging: search BepInEx\LogOutput.log for "[probe]" after a parry.
Client-side only.
